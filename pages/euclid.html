Euclid Class Overview

Euclid-class SCPs are anomalies that require more resources to contain completely or where containment isn't always reliable. This is usually because the SCP is insufficiently understood or inherently unpredictable. Euclid is the largest object class, as most SCPs don't yet fall into any of the other standard classes.

Notable Euclid Class SCPs

SCP-049 EUCLID

Plague Doctor - A humanoid entity resembling a medieval plague doctor. Claims to cure "pestilence" and can transform humans into aggressive zombie-like instances through touch.
