ABOUT THE SCP FOUNDATION

Objectives

The SCP Foundation operates globally to secure anomalous objects, entities, and phenomena; contain them to prevent harm to humanity and the ecosystem; and study them to improve scientific understanding. The Foundation operates clandestinely and has vast resources at its disposal, allowing it to maintain secrecy and normalcy despite the existence of the anomalous.

Departments

The Foundation is divided into numerous departments and divisions, including Containment, Security, Research, Mobile Task Forces, Ethics Committee, and Administrative offices. Each plays a vital role in the Foundation's mission of Secure, Contain, Protect.

Security Clearance

Access to SCP documentation is restricted by security clearance levels. Personnel must undergo rigorous background checks and psychological evaluations. The highest levels of clearance are granted only to a select few individuals who have demonstrated exceptional reliability and psychological fortitude.
