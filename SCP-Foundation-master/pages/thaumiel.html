Thaumiel Class SCPs

Anomalies used by the Foundation to contain other SCPs

Thaumiel Class Overview

Thaumiel-class SCPs are anomalies used by the Foundation to contain or counteract other anomalous threats. These are among the most closely guarded secrets of the Foundation, as their existence is known only to a select few with the highest clearance levels. Thaumiel-class designations are rarely given, as they represent a paradigm shift in the Foundation's containment capabilities.

Notable Thaumiel Class SCPs

SCP-2000 THAUMIEL

Deus Ex Machina - A massive underground facility capable of cloning humanity in the event of an XK-Class end-of-the-world scenario. Uses genetic material preserved from previous cycles to recreate human civilization.

Details
SCP-3000 THAUMIEL

Ananteshesha - A massive, anomalous eel-like entity residing in the Bay of Bengal. Its shed skin has been used to create amnestics and other compounds vital to Foundation operations.

Details
SCP-4231 THAUMIEL

The Montauk House - A location used by the Foundation for critical containment operations. Its anomalous properties have been harnessed to stabilize and contain several high-threat anomalies.
