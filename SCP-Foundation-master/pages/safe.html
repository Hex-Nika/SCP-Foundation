Safe Class SCPs

Objects that are easily and safely contained

Safe Class Overview

Safe-class SCPs are anomalies that are sufficiently understood that their containment does not require significant resources or that the SCP requires a specific and conscious activation or trigger. However, this does not mean that handling or activating it does not pose a threat.

Notable Safe Class SCPs

SCP-173 SAFE

The Sculpture - A concrete statue that moves rapidly when not in direct line of sight. Capable of snapping necks or strangling personnel. Requires constant observation by at least two staff members.

Details
SCP-999 SAFE

The Tickle Monster - A large, orange, amorphous slime mold that induces euphoria and laughter in humans upon contact. It is harmless and seeks only to interact with and comfort humans.

Details
SCP-914 SAFE

The Clockworks - A complex apparatus consisting of various gears, screws, and clock parts arranged around a central electrical device. Capable of refining, degrading, or altering objects placed inside based on five settings: Rough, Coarse, 1:1, Fine, Very Fine.

Details
SCP-261 SAFE

Japanese Vending Machine - A vending machine that dispenses various bizarre snacks and items when Japanese currency is inserted. The items range from harmless to highly anomalous.

Details
