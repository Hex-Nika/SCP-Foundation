Keter Class SCPs

Objects that are exceedingly difficult to contain

Keter Class Overview

Keter-class SCPs are anomalies that are exceedingly difficult to contain consistently or reliably, with containment procedures often being extensive and complex. The Foundation often cannot contain these SCPs well due to not having a solid understanding of the SCP, or lacking the technology to properly contain or counter it. An SCP that is Keter does not mean the SCP is dangerous or that it is Thaumiel; it simply means that it is simple or cost-prohibitive to contain.

Notable Keter Class SCPs

SCP-682 KETER

Hard-to-Destroy Reptile - A large, reptile-like entity of unknown origin. Capable of rapid adaptation to survive any injury or threat. Extremely intelligent and hostile to all life.

The Old Man - A humanoid figure resembling an elderly man capable of passing through solid matter and causing rapid decomposition of organic material it touches.
